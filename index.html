Notes to you

Sync between two phones (tap to expand)

Do this once to pair. Tap "Copy my data", send it to your person, and have them paste it into Import on their phone. This gives you both the same secret, which is what makes your daily codes line up. After that, re-sync whenever you want to share progress.

Apple Health sync. Paste the link of your shared health Gist once (the setup guide explains how to create it and the iPhone Shortcuts that feed it). Steps, calories and sleep then show for both of you on the Health card.

Auto-sync (recommended). With the Gist link above plus your GitHub token here, both phones share everything automatically — tasks, trips, points, spins. The token stays on this phone only; paste the same one on both phones. It's the same token from the Health setup guide.

Push notifications. Get notified when your person adds or finishes something — the setup guide (WeDO-Notifications-Setup.md) walks through creating a free OneSignal account. Paste the App ID (safe to share) and REST API key (stays on this phone, same trust level as your GitHub token above) below, then tap Enable on each phone.

Something looks off? Run this on BOTH phones and compare — it checks the build, sync setup, connections, and trip images, and says exactly what's wrong.

Phone owner. Not set yet. Daily codes only show on their owner's phone. Only change this if it's genuinely wrong.

Lists look empty or wrong? This wipes this phone's data and reloads the starter lists (IKEA list, M&R to-dos, and the RÖoDaY bucket list with your 4 trophies).

Device PIN. Locks this app on this phone so someone picking it up can't read your lists. It does not protect the web address itself — treat the URL as the real secret and don't share it.
